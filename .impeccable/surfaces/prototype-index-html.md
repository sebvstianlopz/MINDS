## Scope
Our own version of the mindsstreet.com storefront (home, collection, product, bag drawer), published privately at its own link. Visitor mode: Persuade. Code-led (no image generation available).

## Audience, job, action
Local Orlando youth (16–30) who follow MINDS on social. Their job: see what's new this season and buy it, or come try it on. Primary actions: add to bag, or get directions to / pick up at MINDS Orlando (The Florida Mall).

## Constraints
English only. Real MINDS catalog, prices, photos and store hours only. Missing photos are shown as labelled "photo needed" slots. Designed freely (user, 22 Nov: "Design freely"); anything Shopify Spotlight can't do natively is listed in DESIGN.md as needing custom code.

## Direction contract
THESIS: MINDS as a campaign studio. Cinematic MINDS photography and tall condensed capitals carry the page, the clothes sit on warm studio stone, and the mantra runs from the hero to the footer. It refuses the stock streetwear grid: a black hero with a centred outline button, then equal white cards.
OWN-WORLD: A warm bone page with stone product tiles (product flats as alpha cut-outs with a soft studio shadow; on-model photos multiplied onto the stone), near-black ink, and MINDS campaign green as the one accent: deep forest for actions and bands, satin green for colour-blocks. Green-black bands and footer. Big Shoulders Display uppercase for display, Archivo for reading. Square corners, hairline rules, outline buttons with arrows.
STORY: The visitor feels the season through the campaign, sees what's new and its price at a glance, believes it's MINDS-made and nearby, then adds to bag or plans the Florida Mall visit.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A split hero under a thin green pickup bar and a bone header with inline nav. On the left, a deep-green panel holds "RESET YOUR MINDSET." in huge bone capitals, one line on the new arrivals, a solid bone "Shop new arrivals" and an outline "Visit The Florida Mall →". On the right is the green-satin campaign portrait at full height. On a phone the photo comes first, then the green panel.
FORM: MINDS Studio; seed: none (pinned). The world is pinned by the user's 22 Nov answers: "MINDS campaign green", "Warm light + cinematic bands", "Tall condensed capitals", "Design freely". The first surface is pinned by the plan the user approved on 22 Nov, which specifies the homepage section by section, opening with "Full-bleed hero: the green satin campaign photo, with huge condensed \"RESET YOUR MINDSET.\" bottom-left, one line of copy, Shop new arrivals (solid) and Visit The Florida Mall → (outline)". Build adaptation, disclosed to the user: the hero is split (forest panel plus a full-height portrait) because the only campaign source is a 1290px portrait that goes soft at full bleed (an owner-blocked asset, see PRODUCT.md).
SIGNATURE: The mantra split across the page: "Reset your mindset." in the hero, "Focus on your vision." on the green-black Femme Noir campaign band, "And the world will be yours." as giant type in the dark footer. Plus a full-bleed row of colour-blocked "Shop by piece" panels with labels and arrows laid over them. Motion grammar (approved 22 Nov motion plan, documented in DESIGN.md → Motion): masked rise for type, hairline draw for rules, curtain unveil for images, scroll-scrub only where position carries meaning, on one easing curve. It is carried by the hero's opposing scroll type, the Femme Noir lights-dim and the footer's outline-to-solid mantra; hover details stay small. Nothing moves under reduced motion.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
