## Scope
Our own version of the mindsstreet.com storefront (home, collection, product, bag drawer), published privately at its own link. Visitor mode: Persuade. Code-led (no image generation available).

## Audience, job, action
Local Orlando youth (16–30) who follow MINDS on social. Their job: see what's new this season and buy it, or come try it on. Primary actions: add to bag, or get directions to / pick up at MINDS Orlando (The Florida Mall).

## Constraints
English only. Real MINDS catalog, prices, photos and store hours only. Missing photos are shown as labelled "photo needed" slots. Designed freely (user, 22 Nov: "Design freely"); anything Shopify Spotlight can't do natively is listed in DESIGN.md as needing custom code.

## Direction contract
THESIS: MINDS as a campaign studio. Cinematic MINDS photography and tall condensed capitals carry the page, the clothes sit on warm studio stone, and the mantra runs from the hero to the footer. It refuses the stock streetwear grid: a black hero with a centred outline button, then equal white cards.
OWN-WORLD: A warm bone page with stone product tiles (flats multiplied onto the stone), near-black ink, and MINDS campaign green as the one accent: deep forest for actions and bands, satin green for colour-blocks. Green-black bands and footer. Big Shoulders Display uppercase for display, Archivo for reading. Square corners, hairline rules, outline buttons with arrows.
STORY: The visitor feels the season through the campaign, sees what's new and its price at a glance, believes it's MINDS-made and nearby, then adds to bag or plans the Florida Mall visit.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A split hero under a thin green pickup bar and a bone header with inline nav. On the left, a deep-green panel holds "RESET YOUR MINDSET." in huge bone capitals, one line on the new arrivals, a solid bone "Shop new arrivals" and an outline "Visit The Florida Mall →". On the right is the green-satin campaign portrait at full height. On a phone the photo comes first, then the green panel.
FORM: MINDS Studio, pinned by the user from their 5 references; seed: none (pinned). User's words (22 Nov answers): "MINDS campaign green", "Warm light + cinematic bands", "Tall condensed capitals", "Design freely".
SIGNATURE: The mantra split across the page: "Reset your mindset." in the hero, "Focus on your vision." on the green-black Femme Noir campaign band, "And the world will be yours." as giant type in the dark footer. Plus a full-bleed row of colour-blocked "Shop by piece" panels with labels and arrows laid over them. Motion grammar: one headline rise on load, a slow image zoom and arrow nudge on hover, and the drawer slide. Nothing under reduced motion.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
