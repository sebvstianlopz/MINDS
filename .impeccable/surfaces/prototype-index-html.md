## Scope
Our own version of the mindsstreet.com storefront (home, collection, product, bag drawer), published privately at its own link. Visitor mode: Persuade. Code-led (no image generation available).

## Audience, job, action
Local Orlando youth (16–30) who follow MINDS on social. Their job: see what's new this season and buy it, or come try it on. Primary actions: add to bag, or get directions to / pick up at MINDS Orlando (The Florida Mall).

## Constraints
English only. Real MINDS catalog, prices, photos and store hours only. No invented deadlines or countdowns. Designed freely; anything Shopify Spotlight can't do natively is listed in DESIGN.md as needing custom code.

## Direction contract
THESIS: MINDS as a quiet lookbook you can shop: photography owns the page edge to edge and the interface steps back to a few words and white pills. It refuses the dense streetwear homepage of bands, badges and colour-blocks.
OWN-WORLD: Warm bone page, concrete-grey product tiles (alpha flats with a soft contact shadow), near-black ink, a pale peach announcement strip, MINDS green only for small signals (bag count, open status, focus). Host Grotesk throughout, display at 600 with tight tracking in sentence case. Pills are the only rounded shape; images, tiles and cards are square and full-bleed with hairline gaps. Dark footer.
STORY: The visitor sees the season in one photograph, picks a category or a new piece at a glance with its price, believes it's MINDS-made and nearby (the Florida Mall card shows live open status), then adds to bag or gets directions.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-screen studio campaign photo under a thin peach pickup strip and a transparent header (white logo, sentence-case nav, icons). Bottom-left: "New season" as a large tight headline, one short line beneath, white pills "Shop now" and "Femme Noir". The header turns solid bone once the photo scrolls away.
FORM: Lookbook storefront; seed: none (pinned). Pinned by the owner's 27 Nov answers: "Clean grotesk, sentence case" and "Full-screen photo, no moving text: Your studio campaign photo fills the screen under a transparent header, with 'New Season' / 'New Arrivals' and two pill buttons in the bottom-left, like the references", plus the approved section-by-section plan. Translation: the craft floor bans eyebrows above headings, so supporting lines sit beneath headings instead.
SIGNATURE: Edge-to-edge category tiles of real MINDS flats on concrete with underlined uppercase labels, and full-bleed 2-up image cards that end in pills. Motion: one calm grammar, a soft fade-rise for sections and the header's transparent-to-solid handover, plus the existing product morph and fly-to-bag; nothing moves under reduced motion.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
