## Scope
Our own version of the mindsstreet.com storefront (homepage, collection, product, cart drawer), published at a separate link. Visitor mode: Persuade. Code-led (no image generation available).

## Audience, job, action
Local Orlando youth (16–30) who follow MINDS on social. Their job: see what's new this season and buy it, or come try it on. Primary actions: add to cart, or get directions to / pick up at MINDS Orlando (The Florida Mall).

## Constraints
Every element must map to a Shopify Spotlight 13 section or setting, Search & Discovery filters, or a Shopify-library font. English only. Real MINDS catalog, prices and photos only; missing photos shown as labelled "photo needed" slots.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The store as MINDS's own notebook, built on its line "Reset your mindset, focus on your vision and the world will be yours". It refuses the stock streetwear template of a black hero with a centered outline button over a product grid.
OWN-WORLD: A plain white page in graphite ink, with one ballpoint-blue accent used only for actions and the mantra. Oswald set bold and uppercase like the logo; Archivo for reading. Hairline graphite rules. Square corners. No shadows except on the cart drawer.
STORY: The visitor sees this season's pieces at once, believes they're MINDS-made and in stock nearby, then buys or plans a visit to the Florida Mall.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed campaign photo, with a white "page" panel anchored bottom-left. The panel holds the H1 "Reset your mindset.", one line on the new arrivals, a solid blue "Shop new arrivals" button and an outline "Visit The Florida Mall" button. Above it sit the announcement bar (Florida Mall pickup in 24h) and an inline menu.
FORM: Mindset Journal, position 5 of 7 on the ranked list; seed:5cdb95f05c915283 (degraded roll, no challengers).
SIGNATURE: The mantra set huge in blue Oswald across a quiet white band, its three clauses stacked, before the visit-the-store section. Motion grammar: one calm fade on route change, hover swaps to the second image, and nothing at all under reduced motion.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
