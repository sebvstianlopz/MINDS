# MINDS photo shot list

The new direction is led by photography, as in the reference sites the owner shared. Most of MINDS's current images are product flats on white. The prototype covers the gap with cut-outs on stone tiles, but these shots would make the real site match its design.

## 1. Campaign hero (most important)
- **What:** one landscape campaign frame, at least **2560 × 1440px**, plus a portrait crop (4:5) for phones.
- **Why:** the current hero (`IMG_3110.png`) is only 1290px wide, so it can't fill a desktop screen without going soft.
- **Look:** keep the current campaign's green-satin and plant world, since it's where the site's accent colour comes from. Use a plain studio wall, soft light, and room on one side for the headline.

## 2. Studio set (for the campaign band and social)
- **Backdrop:** one colour, either deep MINDS green or warm stone, kept the same across the whole set.
- **Each new arrival:** one full-length on-model shot and one waist-up shot.
- **Group shot:** 4–6 people in that season's pieces, standing in a loose line, full-length.
- **Notes:** match the format of the Femme Noir model shots that already exist, which are the best on-model images the store has.

## 3. Product photos (every product)
- **Front flat:** keep the current white-background style, so cut-outs stay easy and clean.
- **Add a back view** for every product.
- **Add a detail close-up** (fabric, stitching, rhinestones, patches) for anything **$100 or more**, starting with the Legacy Croc Jacket, Tracksuit Tops and Recon Cargo.
- **Add an on-model shot** with height and size worn, for example "Model is 5'9", wearing M".

## 4. Stores
- **Florida Mall:** a daytime store-front photo with no mall glare, plus one interior shot. This is the main "visit" image.
- **Wynwood:** a clear store-front. The current photo is cropped through the sign.

## Catalog fixes found while building
- The "Endless Shine Tee" photos are swapped: the product called **blue** shows a **lavender** tee, and the **purple** one shows a **blue** tee.
- Several products share the same name and differ only in colour (Orbit 3/4 Studs, Outlaw Cap, Archive Cap, Femme Noir). Add the colour to each name, or merge them into one product with colour variants.
- Some product links (handles) are random text (`ydb-db`, `ysbudfjlb`). Rename them, for example to `outlaw-cap-black`.
- "Forever Minds Pants" shows a pair of shorts.
